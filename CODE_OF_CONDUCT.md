# Code of conduct

This project is for anyone who uses or builds agent skills. Be the kind of collaborator you would want on your own
project:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, slurs, sexual content or personal attacks, in any project space.
- Keep discussion on the work. Give others' private details only with their consent.
- Credit other people's work, including the authors of the bundled skills.

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people
who keep breaking them. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers through the contact details on the
repository's owner profile.
